Frequently Asked Questions

Home/FAQ
What is LifeLink?▼

LifeLink is a smart blood donor finder platform connecting blood donors, receivers, hospitals, and blood banks across Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh. It helps people find nearby donors quickly during emergencies with instant AI matching and live alerts.

How does the AI Donor Matching work?▼

Our AI Donor Matching engine evaluates 4 key dimensions: ABO & Rh blood compatibility, real-time GPS distance/proximity, active availability status, and last donation eligibility cooldown (90 days) to rank the most suitable donors from top to bottom.

How do I register as a blood donor or receiver?▼

Click on "Register" in the navigation and choose either the "Blood Donor" tab or "Blood Receiver" tab. Donors fill in their blood group and donation history, while receivers can specify their blood group requirement and hospital.

Is LifeLink free to use?▼

Yes! LifeLink is completely free for both donors and blood seekers. We believe saving lives should never have a price tag.

How often can I donate blood?▼

Men can safely donate whole blood every 12 weeks (3 months) and women every 16 weeks (4 months). LifeLink automatically tracks your donation history and calculates your eligibility for AI matching.

Which regions does LifeLink cover?▼

LifeLink specializes across all major cities and districts in Tamil Nadu (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem, Vellore, Tirunelveli, Puducherry) and Andhra Pradesh (Tirupati, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Guntur, Nellore, Kurnool, Kadapa, Rly Kodur, Anantapur).

How can hospitals and blood banks partner with LifeLink?▼

Hospitals can reach out to us via the Contact page or email us at help@lifelink.com. We offer integration for live blood stock management and emergency alerts.

Reach out to our support team. We're available 24/7 across South India.
