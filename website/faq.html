Frequently Asked Questions

Home/FAQ
What is LifeLink?▼

LifeLink is a smart blood donor finder platform that connects blood donors, seekers, hospitals, and blood banks. It helps people find nearby blood donors quickly during emergencies using location-based search and instant alerts.

How do I register as a blood donor?▼

Click on "Register" in the navigation, fill in your personal details including blood group, location, and availability, then submit. You'll be verified by our team and added to the donor network.

Is LifeLink free to use?▼

Yes! LifeLink is completely free for both donors and blood seekers. We believe saving lives should never have a price tag.

How does the emergency request system work?▼

When you create an emergency request, our system automatically identifies matching donors near the specified hospital location and sends them instant push notifications. Donors can then respond, and you can contact them directly.

How often can I donate blood?▼

Men can safely donate whole blood every 12 weeks (3 months) and women every 16 weeks (4 months). LifeLink automatically tracks your donation history and sends reminders when you're eligible to donate again.

How are donors verified?▼

Donors are verified through phone OTP verification, email confirmation, and manual review by our admin team. Verified donors get a checkmark badge on their profile, building trust for seekers.

Can I set my availability status?▼

Yes! You can toggle your availability status between "Available" and "Not Available" from your dashboard. When set to unavailable, you won't receive emergency request notifications.

Is my personal information safe?▼

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption, secure authentication, and strict data privacy policies. Your contact information is only visible to verified seekers with genuine blood requests.

Which cities does LifeLink cover?▼

LifeLink currently operates across 48+ cities in India including Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, and more. We're expanding rapidly!

How can hospitals partner with LifeLink?▼

Hospitals can reach out to us via the Contact page or email us at partners@lifelink.com. We offer free integration with our platform for blood stock management and emergency alerts.

Still have questions?

Reach out to our support team. We're available 24/7.
